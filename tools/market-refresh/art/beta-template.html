Enhanced Matte is compared with sellers using a similar heavyweight matte inkjet paper (Monk Office prints posters on Enhanced Matte; Newfoundland Canvas uses a 230 gsm fine art matte). Luster and Glossy are compared with photo paper prints. Plans are compared with bond prints of the same ink, and Staples by the turnaround you pick.
Reference only. Cotton rag fine art (Posterjack, Newfoundland Canvas) is a premium paper above Enhanced Matte, and poster stock (Staples, VistaPrint) is a cheaper non-giclee paper below it. They are shown so you can see where you sit, but never used for the quote unless you choose the reference option in the settings.
Where the prices come from. Each seller's own online prices on -, before tax and shipping. Sizes between listed ones are estimated by area. Most sellers charge the same per print at any quantity; VistaPrint gives quantity discounts. Posterjack often runs coupon codes; regular prices are used.
What's not in material cost. Waste, test prints, trimming, packaging, file prep and your time. "Over material cost" has to cover those.
