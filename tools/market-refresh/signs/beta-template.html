Like for like. Banners are compared by material weight (13 oz vs heavy 15-18 oz) and by edges (hemmed + grommets vs grommets only). Lawn signs are compared by how they're made, by sides, and by stakes. A seller that differs on any of these is listed as not compared, with the reason.
Vinyl + laminate vs direct print. Every lawn sign seller found (VistaPrint, Staples, Sticker Mule, Kelly Signs) prints straight onto the coroplast with no laminate. Your printed vinyl + laminate signs are a different, longer-lasting product, so their prices are shown for reference only.
Where the prices come from. Each seller's own online prices on -, before tax and before shipping (each seller's shipping is noted). Sizes between captured ones are estimated by area. Staples and Kelly Signs charge the same per sign at any quantity.
What's not in material cost. Waste, grommets, hemming tape, design, setup, cutting, mounting and your time. "Over material cost" has to cover those.
